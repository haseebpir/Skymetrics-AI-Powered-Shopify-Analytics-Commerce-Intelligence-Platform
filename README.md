## ✨ Overview

**Skymetrics** is an intelligent commerce analytics platform designed to help Shopify brands transform high-volume store data into actionable business insights. By combining revenue tracking, product-level performance analysis, multi-region reporting, and conversational AI, the platform gives store owners a centralized workspace for understanding performance and making informed merchandising decisions.

I engineered the interface system and cross-platform front end for Skymetrics, balancing dense unit-economics with a clean, highly scannable dashboard experience. Featuring adaptable light and dark themes, interactive funnel gauges, global regional breakdowns, and an integrated AI assistant, the platform enables merchants to investigate sales velocity, identify cart drop-offs, and access forecasts without leaving their primary dashboard.
